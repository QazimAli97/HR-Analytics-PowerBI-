👥 HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI Project)
🧩 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns and workforce demographics using Power BI. It provides actionable insights into the factors affecting employee turnover, helping HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve retention, engagement, and hiring strategies.

🧠 Business Question

What are the key drivers of employee attrition, and how do age, salary, education, and job role impact turnover rates?

🧮 Data & Analysis

Dataset includes employee records with:

Demographics — Age, Gender, Education Field
Employment — Job Role, Years at Company, Department
Financial — Monthly Income
Attrition Status

Key Metrics:

Total Employees: 1,470
Total Attrition: 237
Attrition Rate: 16.1%
Average Age: 37 years
Average Salary: $6.5K
Average Tenure: 7 years

🧰 Tools & Techniques

Power BI Desktop for visualization and dashboard creation
DAX Measures for attrition rate, averages, and segmentation
Data Modeling to relate demographic, role, and financial data
HR Analytics Framework applied for turnover diagnostics

📈 Power BI Visualizations

KPI Cards: Total Employees, Attrition, Rate, Avg Age, Avg Salary, Avg Years
Donut Chart: Attrition by Education Field (Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, etc.)
Bar Chart: Attrition by Age Group (26–35 highest attrition segment)
Column Chart: Attrition by Salary Range (≤ $5K shows highest turnover)
Stacked Bar: Attrition by Job Role (Lab Technician, Sales Executive, Research Scientist top attrition roles)
Gender Split: Attrition by Gender (140 Male | 79 Female)
Line Chart: Attrition by Years at Company (spikes within first 2–5 years)

🚀 Insights

Overall attrition rate: 16.1%, moderate but improvable.
Employees earning ≤ $5K show the highest attrition, indicating salary dissatisfaction.
Early-tenure employees (0–5 years) account for the majority of exits.
Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives experience the most turnover.
Age group 26–35 is most likely to leave, suggesting mid-career mobility.
Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds dominate overall employee base.

💡 Business Impact

Enables HR to pinpoint high-risk groups for attrition intervention.
Provides visibility into salary and tenure-based retention gaps.
Supports strategic workforce planning and targeted engagement programs.
Drives data-driven policy design to reduce turnover and training costs.
